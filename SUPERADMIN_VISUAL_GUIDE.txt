⭐ WHAT MAKES SUPER ADMIN UNIQUE
================================================================================

Unlike Admin (single org) or Manager (single dept):

✅ MULTI-ORGANIZATION OVERSIGHT
   • Manage multiple organizations
   • View platform-wide analytics
   • Track all subscriptions

✅ SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT
   • Monitor subscription lifecycle
   • Track renewal dates
   • Calculate annual revenue
   • Cost-per-user tracking

✅ CONTENT MANAGEMENT
   • Manage all training modules
   • Update platform-wide content
   • Track module performance

✅ PLATFORM ANALYTICS
   • See all organizations' data
   • Monitor completion rates across platform
   • Identify top-performing modules
   • Revenue and cost tracking

✅ EXCLUSIVE FEATURES
   • Organization management (add, edit, delete)
   • Subscription renewals
   • Revenue reports
   • Platform billing
   • Content library management
